## E002 — Creator documentary summarized by professional media

- Class: P1/S1 — developer documentary reported by professional media
- Sources:
  - AUTOMATON, 2025-04-12: https://automaton-media.com/articles/newsjp/repo-20250412-334967/
  - PC Gamer, 2025: https://www.pcgamer.com/games/horror/lets-just-fail-quickly-this-time-semiwork-took-a-big-risk-on-repo-after-its-first-game-took-6-years-to-make-and-didnt-sell-very-well/
  - semiwork official Steam announcement linking `How R.E.P.O. was CREATED!`

Reported creator account:
- Voidigo took about six years and left the team exhausted;
- it was not commercially successful enough to safely guarantee another project;
- the team took the money made from Voidigo and reinvested it into the next game;
- nobody at the studio had previously used Unity;
- an early found-footage / haunted-house-cleaning prototype became Project Clean;
- multiplayer made cleaning feel poor, leading to repeated redesigns toward moving/tax-collection/value extraction;
- the team explicitly framed the new approach around failing faster than the six-year previous cycle.

Supports: C004, C011.

Boundary:
- media transcription of a creator video is stronger than ordinary secondary reporting but still should be timecoded against the original video in the next pass.
- “all the money” is creator rhetoric, not audited finance.
