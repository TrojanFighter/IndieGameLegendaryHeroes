- Claim use:
  - founders left Tencent-era stable employment and began from a three-person core prototype;
  - early single-player PVE prototype exposed a severe content-cost problem after roughly three months: producing 5–7 minutes at target quality was described as costing close to RMB100k;
  - team pivoted to PvP and later adopted clearer commercial benchmarks including a hero-shooter / Ghost-Recon-like direction;
  - retrospective says the team at points did not understand the project's core problem and repeatedly changed direction;
  - after seven years, producer emphasizes running the core loop end-to-end, disciplined project management and deadlines;
  - producer says that with later knowledge he would not directly choose a PvP space-shooter project; if still choosing it, he would do more planning/validation first.
- Confidence: HIGH for the producer's retrospective testimony; MEDIUM for exact recollected historical cost figure.
- Boundary:
  - this is retrospective testimony written after failure and can rationalize history;
  - the cost number is not an audited budget;
  - it does not prove any single pivot caused commercial failure.
