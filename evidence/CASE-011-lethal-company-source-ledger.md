## E001 — Push to Talk: 10-year journey

- Class: P1/S1 — researched profile containing direct creator interview
- Source: Ryan K. Rigney, `The 10-Year Journey Behind Lethal Company’s Success`, 2024-01-19
- URL: https://www.pushtotalk.gg/p/how-lethal-company-sold-10-million-copies

Reported / creator-backed chronology:
- Zeekerss began publicly logging Roblox-related creation around age ten in 2012.
- The profile reconstructs 12 Roblox releases over roughly four years and later Unity/itch/Steam releases.
- It describes Lethal Company as the 20th release, after 19 earlier games/experiments.
- Patreon development logging began in 2022; Zeekerss says the journal captured a late-2022 design crisis and major scrapping.
- small playtests were underway by August 2023.
- SteamDB launch-growth reconstruction shows weekly CCU increasing from hundreds to thousands to tens of thousands rather than a single instantaneous spike.

Boundary:
- “20 games” depends on what counts as a release versus demo/experiment.
- sales/revenue estimates in the article are third-party estimates, not audited financials.

## E002 — Zeekerss Patreon: Looking back + major progress

- Class: P0 — contemporaneous creator devlog
- Source: Zeekerss, `Looking back + major progress`, 2023-06-22
- URL: https://www.patreon.com/zeekerss/posts/looking-back-84963141

Creator states:
- he knew online development would be hard;
- the reason he felt able to attempt it was momentum gained while making The Upturned;
- much of the development difficulty involved discovering the game's identity, not only learning netcode;
- playtests were making the project feel like a real game rather than a playground.

Supports:
- C011: immediate predecessor directly supplied confidence/capability.
- C004: project identity was actively redefined during production.
