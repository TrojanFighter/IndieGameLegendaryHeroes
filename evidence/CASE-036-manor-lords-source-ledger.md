## E001 — Greg Styczeń direct interview: hobby origin, day job, grant and outsourcing

- Source class: P1/P0 — direct developer interview immediately before Early Access
- Title: Solo dev makes sophisticated sim Manor Lords using Unreal Engine
- Author / Institution: Brian Crecente / Unreal Engine; interviewee Greg Styczeń
- Published: 2024-04-25
- Accessed: 2026-10-06
- URL: https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/solo-dev-makes-sophisticated-sim-manor-lords-using-unreal-engine
- Claim use:
  - Greg began game-making young and describes Manor Lords as his first serious project, not first game-making experience;
  - early Manor Lords was a hobby/part-time project while he worked as a video freelancer;
  - Patreon and Epic MegaGrant preceded professional outsourcing;
  - MegaGrant let him stop taking day-job commissions and focus full time;
  - he hired freelance artists for tasks such as mocap cleanup and event illustrations;
  - Greg says more people do not magically speed development but can quickly raise production value;
  - Falling Frontier developer Todd D'Arcy introduced him to Hooded Horse CEO Tim Bender;
  - Greg describes Unreal/ready-made tooling as crucial to solo-core feasibility.
- Confidence: HIGH for Greg's stated production history
- Boundary: interview is self-report and does not provide audited grant amount, budget or full contractor list.

## Current evidence-level conclusions

### STRONGLY SUPPORTED

- Greg was the long-term solo/permanent core, but not the entire production perimeter;
- early runway came from part-time freelance video work;
- Patreon and Epic MegaGrant enabled full-time focus and professional outsourcing;
- freelancers, assets, QA and publisher labor materially contributed;
- publisher market-access labor was an explicit part of the system;
- organization expanded after Early Access success.

### SUPPORTED / NEEDS MORE GRANULARITY

- non-dilutive funding had unusually high leverage because it converted author time and specialist perimeter simultaneously;
- retaining a one-person permanent core preserved authorial consistency while outsourcing narrow production functions;
- Hooded Horse materially improved market access, though exact causal magnitude is unknown.
