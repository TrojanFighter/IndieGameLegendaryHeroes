## E001 — Chris Hunt interview on eight-year development

- Class: P1 — direct creator interview
- Source: Siliconera, `Kenshi’s Eight Year Development Journey From One-Man RPG To A Team’s Success`, 2015
- URL: https://www.siliconera.com/KENSHIS-EIGHT-YEAR-DEVELOPMENT-JOURNEY-FROM-ONE-MAN-RPG-TO-A-TEAMS-SUCCESS/

Chris Hunt states:
- he had started Kenshi around eight years earlier;
- for the first five or six years he worked alone on it full-time while juggling a minimum-wage night security job to survive;
- much of that time went into fundamental systems and reaching a playable state;
- after Steam Early Access he could afford additional manpower;
- over roughly two years he built a small team including programming, world design, PR/writing and art roles.

Supports:
- C002: sustainable recurring income can be more important than a single financing event.
- C003/C011: the later studio form rests on years of solo foundation.

## E002 — Lo-Fi Games official history / fact sheet

- Class: P1 — official institutional retrospective
- Source: Lo-Fi Games, `Fact Sheet`
- URL: https://lofigames.com/press/

Official history states:
- Hunt spent his early twenties working as a game programmer;
- in 2008 he left and worked on Kenshi full-time while doing night security shifts to scrape by;
- after Steam Greenlight / Early Access in 2013, Kenshi raised enough money to fund a small team.

Supports:
- professional programming prehistory;
- salary cross-subsidy → market revenue → team expansion sequence.

Boundary:
- does not give exact wages, savings, EA revenue or total budget.
