## Current evidence-level conclusions

### STRONGLY SUPPORTED
- Outer Wilds began as a USC thesis/student project with a clear design thesis before commercial-scale finance.
- Mobius absorbed and staffed the project while retaining original creative core.
- team capability materially expanded after studio adoption.
- Fig funding and later Annapurna/Xbox/Epic partnerships supported the project.
- developer explicitly connected those partnerships to keeping the studio alive long enough to ship at desired quality.
- Epic launch distribution produced backer/stakeholder controversy.

### SUPPORTED WITH BOUNDARY
- Outer Wilds is a strong external-capital CAPABILITY-EXPANDED case.
- external capital expanded the stakeholder surface around production/distribution.

### UNKNOWN / DO NOT INFER
- exact publisher/platform funding amounts;
- exact IP / ownership / recoup / approval rights;
- whether Epic exclusivity was contractually required by any one partner;
- total budget;
- audited ROI;
- exact counterfactual scope without external capital.
