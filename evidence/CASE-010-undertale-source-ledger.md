# Evidence Ledger — CASE-010 Undertale / Toby Fox

- Case: `CASE-010`
- Status: ACTIVE
- Last updated: 2026-10-03

## E001 — Undertale Kickstarter

- Class: P0 — contemporaneous primary
- Source: Toby Fox, `UnderTale`, Kickstarter, 2013
- URL: https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/1002143342/undertale

Campaign facts:
- Goal: USD 5,000.
- Pledged: USD 51,124.
- Backers: 2,398.
- Funding period: 2013-06-24 — 2013-07-24.

Supports:
- Crowdfunding materially exceeded the stated goal and became an explicit production resource.

Does not support:
- total development budget = $51,124;
- crowdfunding was the first source of audience or capability.

## E002 — 2013 developer-to-developer interview

- Class: P0/P1 — near-contemporaneous direct creator interview
- Source: `Toby Fox’s Undertale – DEV 2 DEV INTERVIEW #1`, 2013-05-25
- URL: https://meloshantani.wordpress.com/2013/05/25/toby-foxs-undertale-dev-2-dev-interview-1/

Toby states:
- around 2000 he and his three brothers used RPG Maker 2000 to make RPGs, though almost none were finished or released;
- he made several EarthBound ROM hacks in high school;
- Undertale was his first “REAL” game project;
- he started composing for Homestuck in late 2009.

Supports:
- C011: formal project start radically undercounts creative/technical prehistory.
- C006: no AAA job did not mean zero prior capability.

## E003 — Famitsu 2017 interview

- Class: P1 — direct creator retrospective
- Source: Famitsu, `『UNDERTALE』の開発者トビー・フォックス氏に聞く`, 2017-07-12
- URL: https://www.famitsu.com/news/201707/12137087.html

Toby states:
- in December 2012, as a third-year university student, he began building a text display system and then a battle system;
- while building the system, story ideas emerged;
- he found Temmie Chang's blog and directly asked her to collaborate;
- the demo was completed in early 2013, followed by Kickstarter.

Supports:
- student-status chronology;
- Temmie is a real collaborator, not an incidental later credit;
- system prototype preceded the full story plan.

## E004 — Automaton 2015 interview

- Class: P1 — direct creator interview near release
- Source: AUTOMATON, `2D-RPG『Undertale』開発者Tobyfoxインタビュー`, 2015
- URL: https://automaton-media.com/articles/interviewsjp/undertale-developer-toby-fox-interview/

Toby states:
- he had used RPG Maker / RPG Maker 2000 since around age ten for small private joke games;
- before Undertale, his strongest public credential was composing for Homestuck from 2009 onward.

Supports:
- long prehistory in low-cost creation and music.

## E005 — Toby Fox 2025 newsletter on Homestuck debt

- Class: P1 — creator retrospective
- Source: Toby Fox, `UNDERTALE / DELTARUNE Newsletter — Fall 2025`
- URL: https://darkgodteam.github.io/eng/newsletters/autumn25/index.html

Toby says Homestuck:
- forged him into the composer he became;
- gave him a social-media following that greatly helped when launching Undertale's Kickstarter;
- indirectly connected him to Temmie because he first noticed her Homestuck fanart.

Supports:
- capability capital and market/social capital came from the same prehistory.
- “no marketing” would erase a real audience network.

## Current evidence-level conclusions

### STRONGLY SUPPORTED
- Undertale was Toby's first major completed/released game, not his first attempt at game creation.
- RPG Maker, ROM hacks and Homestuck preceded the formal project.
- Kickstarter materially exceeded its goal.
- Temmie Chang was a meaningful collaborator.
- Homestuck contributed both compositional capability and audience/network access.

### UNKNOWN
- exact student living runway;
- total cost / opportunity cost;
- complete contributor map;
- quantitative causal weight of preexisting following versus later organic spread.
