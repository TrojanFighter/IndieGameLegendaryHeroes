## Current evidence-level conclusions

### STRONGLY SUPPORTED
- Barth had substantial programming/engineering/game-making prehistory before SpaceChem.
- engineering/problem-solving knowledge directly shaped recurring game subject matter and mechanics.
- SpaceChem was developed with a day job, small cash budget and distributed contributors.
- TIS-100 emerged partly from deleting art/content obligations that made another concept too expensive.
- Zachtronics deliberately targeted a programmer/engineering niche and repeatedly built for it.
- the studio eventually described its own strong specialization as limiting its ability to make different kinds of games.

### SUPPORTED WITH BOUNDARY
- FIT-LOCK-IN / capability path dependence is a plausible formal reading of the studio's 2022 self-description.
- built-in shareable outputs such as Opus Magnum GIFs created market coherence around the same system capability.

### UNKNOWN / DO NOT INFER
- exact profitability of each title;
- exact Ironclad Tactics loss;
- lifetime payroll/burn;
- whether specialization statistically reduced studio longevity;
- whether Coincidence has meaningfully escaped the same capability path.
