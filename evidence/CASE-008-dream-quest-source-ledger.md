## E001 — 2014 Peter Whalen long-form interview

- Class: P1 — direct creator interview near release
- Source: Jesse Fuchs, `Those Are My Demographics: Kids Learning How To Read, and Game Designers`, 2014-10-06
- URL: https://medium.com/@jessefuchs/those-are-my-demographics-kids-who-are-just-learning-to-read-and-game-designers-97651de9e15

Direct creator statements:
- Dream Quest was his first game released to the public, but he had made games when younger.
- He had a graduate mathematics/combinatorics background and a long history with Magic and making custom cards.
- Before Dream Quest he had already spent about a year on a more sophisticated digital card game, giving him a basic engine.
- Once the Dream Quest concept emerged, a dungeon prototype/script took about a week.
- Despite that head start, he estimates roughly six months to feature complete, then another six to eight months of testing, tweaking and balance work.
- He had tried working with artists but those collaborations did not persist; he preferred to retain abstraction.
- He spent time searching for royalty-free music and bought a few inexpensive tracks.
- The interviewer characterizes the game as spreading slowly through boardgame/game-design communities; Whalen had a small Twitter following but unusually high designer concentration.

Supports:
- C011: prototype time is radically shorter than capability/product time.
- C003/C007: solo implementation relies on prior tools and deliberately low representation cost.
- C010: person-to-person professional/community diffusion is still market access.

Boundary:
- A single long creator interview does not establish income/runway or sales.
- “First public release” must not be rewritten as “first game ever made.”

## E002 — Author corpus provenance: Dream Quest + Gunpoint

- Class: H — author-source lead, not independent factual verification
- Source: 洪荒行者, 《正在到来的中度数值通胀率游戏设计革命》 / 游戏炼乳早期文章汇编

Use:

## Current evidence-level conclusions

### STRONGLY SUPPORTED
- Dream Quest had substantial prehistory in card-game expertise and an existing digital-card engine.
- “one week” refers to an early playable/prototype stage, not completed production.
- Product iteration continued roughly another year or more.

### UNKNOWN
- Runway / living support.
- Sales and income.
- Exact causal path into Blizzard employment.
