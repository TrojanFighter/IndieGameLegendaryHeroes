## Current evidence assessment

### STRONGLY SUPPORTED

- Tyroller's major shipped work spans a three-person team, solo-core project and two-person team;
- ISLANDERS and Thronefall must be credited as collaborative work rather than Jonas-only achievements;
- WYS took roughly four years and involved community contributors beyond the solo core;
- Thronefall had multiple discarded prototypes, reached EA after ~12 months and targeted ~24 months total;
- Thronefall deliberately simplified the fantasy of classic RTS games rather than reproducing full RTS complexity;
- interview-level evidence reports roughly 1M Thronefall copies in its first year;
- Tyroller remains an active practitioner and source/interviewer in 2025–2026.

### SUPPORTED WITH BOUNDARY

- repeated success is consistent with prototype discipline, scope compression, market legibility and capability accumulation;
- public communication plausibly contributes to market access and learning, but its causal sales contribution is not yet quantified;
- current `fantasy first` language may summarize an older implicit practice, but that longitudinal continuity is still being tested.

### UNKNOWN / OPEN

- audited title-by-title net revenue / profit;
- exact WYS lifetime sales and contributor perimeter;
- exact ISLANDERS original development/budget/sales timeline;
- quantitative YouTube audience → wishlist / sales conversion;
