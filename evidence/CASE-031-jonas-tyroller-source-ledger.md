### STRONGLY SUPPORTED

- Tyroller's major shipped work spans a three-person team, solo-core project and two-person team;
- ISLANDERS and Thronefall must be credited as collaborative work rather than Jonas-only achievements;

- public communication plausibly contributes to market access and learning, but its causal sales contribution is not yet quantified;
- current `fantasy first` language may summarize an older implicit practice, but that longitudinal continuity is still being tested.
