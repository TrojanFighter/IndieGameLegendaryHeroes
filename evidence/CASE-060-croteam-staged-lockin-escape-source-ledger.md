## Evidence-level verdict

### STRONGLY SUPPORTED
- decade-plus shooter/engine specialization by 2012;
- original Talos split out of incompatible new Serious Sam 4 mechanics;
- preservation of engine/editor/first-person production capital;
- addition of narrative specialists and new puzzle validation/feedback/automation capability;
- significant 2014 public alpha cuts and corrections;
- 2014 release and strong critical acclaim with later sequels;
- 2020 Devolver acquisition;
- 2023 UE5 migration after explicit internal-engine cost-benefit assessment.

### SUPPORTED WITH BOUNDARY
- Croteam is the first strong positive mature-product-grammar pivot comparator in this corpus.
- It illustrates staged product-grammar and toolchain decoupling rather than simultaneous wholesale reinvention.
- It supports a positive counterexample against treating FIT-LOCK-IN as inevitable destiny.

- absolute profitability or total units of original Talos;
- real audience churn/replacement from Serious Sam;
- Devolver's 2013 advance, milestone, recoup or product veto terms;
- total 2014 contributor/periphery ledger and headcount;
- exact comparative costs of rebuilding Serious Engine vs migrating UE5;
- cost of switch under an independent 2014 capital regime;
- automatic cross-studio generalizability.
