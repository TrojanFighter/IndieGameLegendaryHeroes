## Current evidence-level conclusions

### STRONGLY SUPPORTED
- GRIS project thesis existed before Nomada Studio as an organization.
- Roset's visual-author capability and Cuevas/Mendoza's technical/AAA capability were complementary at formation.
- the founding team built a demo before publisher-facing expansion.
- product form and labor allocation were intentionally art-heavy.
- 2D/side-scroller form was partly chosen because it fit Roset's background and pacing goals.
- AAA production capability transferred into a non-AAA objective function.

### SUPPORTED WITH BOUNDARY
- external financing materially expanded production capability, but exact funding source/contract/perimeter remain partially unresolved.
- public team-size figures range from roughly 17 to roughly 26 depending on source/definition; use as perimeter range, not a single audited headcount.

### UNKNOWN / DO NOT INFER
- exact Devolver advance / recoup / IP / milestone terms;
- exact founder runway before the demo;
- audited peak headcount;
- employee vs contractor split;
- Neva production budget and retained-earnings share.
