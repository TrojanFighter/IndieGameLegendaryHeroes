## E001 — GameCloud 2017 creator interview

- Class: P0/P1 — contemporaneous direct creator interview
- Source: Nick Ballantyne, `Project Wingman Interview with Creator "RB-D2" Abi Rahmani`, 2017-12-02
- URL: https://gamecloud.net.au/features/interviews/pgf2017-project-wingman

Abi Rahmani states:
- As of November 2017 he had worked on Project Wingman for exactly two years.
- Before it, he had only made a tiny Unreal Engine 4 prototype that became Project Wingman.
- Major challenges were learning coding, 3D modelling, animation, texturing, marketing and publishing.
- Internet learning material made this skill acquisition possible.
- He had published limited-content alphas publicly.
- Community members volunteered spare time for smaller development aspects and fast feedback.
- He explicitly says it was **not a full one-man effort**.
- Composer Jose Pavli was already a named collaborator.

Supports:
- C003/C011: visible small-team form hides a learning prehistory and contributors.
- C008: common engine / internet knowledge / online distribution reduce some entry barriers.
- C010: public alphas and community interaction are market/feedback infrastructure.

## E002 — Project Wingman Kickstarter

- Class: P0 — contemporaneous primary
- Source: Abi Rahmani (RB-D2), `Project Wingman`, Kickstarter, 2018
- URL: https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/rb-d2/project-wingman

Campaign facts:
- Goal: AU$35,000.
- Pledged: AU$114,544.
- Backers: 2,467.
- Funding period: 2018-05-29 — 2018-07-03.

Supports:
- crowdfunding became a significant mid-development runway / demand signal.

Does not support:
- total project budget = AU$114,544;
- Kickstarter was the first market validation;
- final production team = one or three people.

## E003 — GameTomo 2018 developer interview

- Class: P0/P1 — contemporaneous direct creator interview
- Source: GameTomo, `The Developer of Project Wingman is here with us!`, 2018
- URL: https://www.gametomo.co.jp/news-en/2018/8/31/the-developer-of-project-wingman-is-here-with-us

Creator statements:
- Unreal Engine 4's features and large community materially ease development.
- Rahmani tries to minimize external art assets because he enjoys creating models/textures himself.

Companion Japanese interview:
- https://www.gametomo.co.jp/news-jp/2018/9/10/project-wingman
- Rahmani says the project remained “mostly” solo but Jose Pavli handled music and Matt helped with story supervision / voice actor coordination.

Supports:
- technology access matters, but does not eliminate specialized collaborator needs.
- “solo” must be field-by-field rather than label-based.

## E004 — Origin interview: learning prototype → public traction

- Class: P0/P1 — contemporaneous direct creator interview
- Source: Gearburn/Memeburn, `Q&A: Project Wingman dev on launch window, origins and more`, 2018-01-22
- URL: https://memeburn.com/gearburn/2018/01/interview-project-wingman-dev/

Rahmani says:
- he initially used Unreal to learn the engine, art side and general 3D implementation;
- he created a small prototype and released it publicly;
- it gained traction, so he continued development.

Supports:
- the project was selected for continuation by external response after an exploratory learning prototype.

## Current evidence-level conclusions

### STRONGLY SUPPORTED
- Project Wingman grew out of a UE4 learning prototype.
- Public alphas / feedback predated Kickstarter.

- Final production was never literally one-person.

### SUPPORTED
