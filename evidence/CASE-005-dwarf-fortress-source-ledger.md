# Evidence Ledger — CASE-005 Dwarf Fortress / Bay 12 Games

- Case: `CASE-005`
- Status: ACTIVE
- Last updated: 2026-10-03

## E001 — Bay 12 official Patreon About

- Class: P1 — creator-owned institutional retrospective
- Source: Bay 12 Games, Patreon `About`
- URL: https://www.patreon.com/bay12games/about

Bay 12 states:
- Dwarf Fortress work dates to 2002; first public release was 2006;
- text graphics allow them to add more content than they otherwise could manage;
- Bay 12 has been their full-time job since 2007 because of fan support;
- Tarn gave up his math-professor job to focus on the game;
- player contributions became their sole source of income.

Supports: C002, C004, C007, C010, C011.

## E002 — Game Developer 2008 Tarn Adams interview

- Class: P1 — direct creator interview
- Source: John Harris, `Interview: The Making Of Dwarf Fortress`, Game Developer, 2008-02-27
- URL: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/interview-the-making-of-dwarf-fortress

Tarn describes Dwarf Fortress as the convergence of two long development tracks; one reaches back to an elementary-school BASIC fantasy game.

Supports:
- C011: 2002 project start undercounts ability/design prehistory.

## E003 — PC Gamer 2010 creator interview

- Class: P1 — direct creator interview
- Source: PC Gamer retrospective/interview with Tarn Adams, 2010
- URL: https://www.pcgamer.com/the-making-of-dwarf-fortress/

Creator account reported:
- around the 2006 release period he expected to spend savings for a limited time and then return to employment if necessary;
- after release, monthly donations quickly made continued full-time development plausible and then increased.

Supports:
- donations transformed downside/runway after market validation.

Boundary:
- exact monthly/annual income needs stronger primary accounting records.

## E004 — Bay 12 2019 Steam / itch announcement

- Class: P1 — creator-owned official announcement
- Source: Bay 12 Games, `Dwarf Fortress coming to Steam and itch.io`, 2019
- URL: https://www.patreon.com/bay12games/posts/dwarf-fortress-25343688

Bay 12 states:
- crowdfunding/fan support remained the main source of income before premium release;
- Kitfox made the graphics effort possible;
- artists were already working while Bay 12 handled core code.

Supports:
- premium-era product has a larger contributor/business perimeter than the historical two-person core.

## Current evidence-level conclusions

### STRONGLY SUPPORTED

### UNKNOWN
