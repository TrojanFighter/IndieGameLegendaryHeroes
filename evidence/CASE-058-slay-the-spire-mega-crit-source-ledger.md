## Current evidence-level conclusions

### STRONGLY SUPPORTED
- Casey and Anthony had a shared hobby-game history before their post-college jobs.
- Casey worked in Amazon QA for roughly four years and did not describe the experience as simply oppressive.
- Anthony maintained strong card/board-game taste and wrote the StS design document while employed in software.
- commitment increased in stages: Casey exit → prototype → Anthony full-time exit.
- early playtesting/telemetry and later Early Access/community iteration were central production mechanisms.
- initial market performance was not a breakout; market legibility was built over time.

### SUPPORTED WITH BOUNDARY
- Mega Crit is a strong `Parallel Authorial Thread` case.
- large-company/software experience contributed portable capability without obviously dictating product objective.
- the case is a useful counterpoint to Prestige-Preserving Project Distortion.

### UNKNOWN / DO NOT INFER
- exact savings/runway at resignation;
- household financial support;
- whether Amazon side-project/IP policy was decisive in the original 2015 exit;
- exact Humble publishing economics;
- whether US/Amazon culture generally preserves authorship better than Chinese firms;
- national-level causal claims about meritocracy and indie creation.
