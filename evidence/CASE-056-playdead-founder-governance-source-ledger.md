## Current evidence-level conclusions

### STRONGLY SUPPORTED
- Jensen's LIMBO thesis and visual/creative work preceded Patti and the full studio.
- Patti joined in 2006 with programming/production experience and became central to organizational expansion, financing, hiring and execution.
- Jensen remained the game-direction / authorial center.
- the founder pairing is therefore a real complementary-capability structure rather than post-hoc labeling.
- the pair shipped LIMBO and later INSIDE, so product success did not prevent founder-governance dissolution.
- Patti exited nearly half ownership for DKK 50m after a severe founder conflict.
- Patti directly acknowledges fallout and future development horizon as relevant dimensions.
- authorship / credit conflict persisted into later legal dispute.

### SUPPORTED WITH BOUNDARY
- Playdead is a strong pressure case against reading CAPABILITY-COMPOSED as "find a complementary cofounder and the gap is solved."
- cofounder capability should be accounted for together with equity, authority, authorship, time horizon, deadlock and exit.
- product success can postpone rather than eliminate founder-governance costs.

### UNKNOWN / DO NOT INFER
- exact shareholder/voting agreement before 2016;
- exact ownership percentages at every historical stage;
- exact legal reasoning of Danish Business Authority intervention;
- which founder initiated or caused the breakdown;
- whether project cadence was the primary cause or one dimension of a broader conflict;
- fairness of the DKK 50m valuation;
- final merits/outcome of the 2025–2026 lawsuit;
- whether a specific contractual mechanism could have prevented the split.
