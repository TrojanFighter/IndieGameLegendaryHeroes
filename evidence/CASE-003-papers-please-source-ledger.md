## E001 — Lucas Pope development log / postmortem entries

- Class: P0/P1 — contemporaneous creator devlog and near-release postmortem
- Source: Lucas Pope, `Development Logs by Lucas Pope — Papers, Please`, 2012–2013
- URLs:
  - https://dukope.com/devlogs/papers-please/tig-10/
  - https://dukope.com/devlogs/papers-please/tig-06/
  - https://dukope.com/devlogs/papers-please/tig-05/

Creator record establishes:
- March 2013 beta covered the first eight days;
- after the last beta, Pope filled out mechanics/story and used custom tools to manage progression/economy;
- he explicitly says an important AAA lesson was that nothing is above being cut;
- baggage inspection, booth threats, UV seals and other features were removed when implementation cost outweighed payoff;
- April 2013 he actively produced a trailer and prepared Steam Greenlight rather than treating distribution as separate from production.

Supports: C004, C007, C010, C011.

## E002 — Famitsu 2014 direct interview

- Class: P1 — direct creator retrospective close to release
- Source: Famitsu, `『Papers,Please』のルーカス・ポープ氏を直撃`, 2014-04-04
- URL: https://www.famitsu.com/news/201404/04051083.html

Pope states:
- total development was about nine months;
- first four months were developed publicly with feedback leading to a public beta;
- original plan was to finish in six months because he expected a small game with modest sales;
- Steam Greenlight and media attention convinced him to add roughly three months of polish and stop publishing the devlog during the final push.

Supports:
- market validation changed production commitment;
- “9 months” includes a deliberate extension after external evidence.
