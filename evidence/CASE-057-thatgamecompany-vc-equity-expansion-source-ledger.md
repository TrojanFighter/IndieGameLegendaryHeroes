## Current evidence-level conclusions

### STRONGLY SUPPORTED
- TGC's Sony era involved three-game exclusivity, Sony ownership of the games, project funding and recoup-before-royalty economics according to Chen.
- Benchmark invested $5.5M in 2012 and Mitch Lasky joined TGC's board.
- the 2012 strategic goal was independent/multiplatform future development and publishing.
- Chen later explicitly said VC did not give him creative input after backing the pitched vision.
- an additional $7M in 2014 was explicitly tied to scaling development and building self-publishing / marketing / distribution infrastructure.
- by Chen's 2024 account, the organization expanded from roughly 15 people after early financing to roughly 40 at Sky launch and added customer-service / marketing / PR functions.
- by the same account, additional financing rounds were necessary and fundraising repeatedly disrupted development.

### SUPPORTED WITH BOUNDARY
- thatgamecompany is a strong `VC-EQUITY / CAPABILITY-EXPANDED` anchor.
- equity financing shifted part of the control surface from project/publisher/platform contracts to company-level shareholder/board governance.
- equity capital bought organizational capabilities beyond game development itself.

### UNKNOWN / DO NOT INFER
- exact cap table or valuation for 2012/2014 rounds;
- investor ownership percentages;
- liquidation preferences, protective provisions, pro-rata or veto terms;
- exact board composition over time;
- whether any investor could approve/reject specific game features;
- exact spending allocation;
- exact terms/amounts of all three later fundraising rounds Chen referenced;
- a single-cause explanation for Sky's seven-year development.
