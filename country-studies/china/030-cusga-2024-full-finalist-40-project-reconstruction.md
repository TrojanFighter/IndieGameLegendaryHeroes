## 3. Special audit rules learned in this transcription

**Repeated nomination != separate game:** four exact title+team pairs receive two category slots. Do not publish `44 finalist games`.

**Same team != same game:** 「虚无幻象」 appears for *Spirit* and *Van宁弹射物语*, which are **different published titles**. This requires team/account and project/creative-work to be distinct IDs; never deduplicate solely by team.

**Official stage != general population:** 40 projects are both self-selected participants and selected finalists of a 500+ submitted works competition. They cannot serve as the ordinary college student's game-writing probability. The earlier aim of recovering **all 75 prelim entrants** remains valuable, but 75 itself is still selected, not total submissions.

**Known release from another linked person != continuation of baseline game:** 《鼠鼠来了》alumni later created a different title; it must not be counted as `SAME_PROJECT_SHIPPED`, although it can be `TEAM_MEMBER_NEW_AUTHORIAL_PROJECT` if directly linked by interviews. No special status is assigned for this 40/40 snapshot so outcomes remain truly precommitted.

**Absolute region:** CUSGA official 2024 source included students from HK/Macau/Taiwan and overseas institutions; don't call these 40 all Mainland-born students.

**Cannot use names alone for later Steam matching:** match organiser public game title, developer/team identity, gameplay/artifact, and official lineage. See [AGENTS](../../AGENTS.md) `TITLE_COLLISION_EXCLUDED` rule established after Taiwan 2025 'Backpack Dungeon' false match.
