# CASE-061 — Supergiant / Bastion：来自 EA 的团队，拒绝以 EA 的生产组织证明自己

- Case ID: CASE-061
- Subject: Amir Rao / Gavin Simon / Greg Kasavin / early Supergiant
- Period covered: EA Command & Conquer work → 2009 independent founding → 2011 Bastion → 2019 longitudinal check
- Research status: RESEARCHING
- Related Claims: []
- Corpus role: `AAA-SKILL-TRANSFER / OBJECTIVE-FUNCTION-DECOUPLING / CAPABILITY-SHAPED-TEAM / HOUSEHOLD-SUBSIDY / IP-CONTROL`
- Evidence Ledger: [E001–E006](../evidence/CASE-061-supergiant-bastion-aaa-to-author-source-ledger.md)

## 1. Myth

- **False A:** “有 EA AAA 经历，必然用 AAA 流程做独立第一作。”
- **False B:** “敢辞职、有七个人就够做 Bastion。”
- **False C:** “Warner Bros. 投资买出 Bastion。”2011 Amir Rao 明确称 WB 是发行/营销及认证渠道伙伴，**并没有提供游戏开发资金**；具体发行成本与商业合同未公开。
- **False D:** “欧美大厂老兵离职自然会保留作者性。”本案不能推导国家/文化总体差异。

## 2. Context–Situation–Action Snapshot

### Era / Production Regime
2009–2011：XBLA / Steam 等数字发行降低中等规模游戏的实体渠道障碍；媒体、PAX 10、IGF 和 E3 等线下/发行事件仍是重要 market interface。彼时不存在 2026 的 AI/工具成本结构。

### Actor Situation
- Amir Rao 与 Gavin Simon 均曾参与 EA LA `Command & Conquer`；Greg Kasavin 同样在 EA LA 与两人共事。
- Rao 长期组织桌面角色扮演游戏，早年已有 company-external taste / social creative network，不是离职当天才拥有作者性。
- 两位创始人从 EA 退出，自筹现金，在 Rao 父亲于 San Jose 的住宅客厅工作；父亲对失败后重新就业持较宽容态度。这是明确的 household/space subsidy。
- 早期为约七人的多专业互补团队：Jen Zee 美术、Darren Korb 音乐、Logan Cunningham 配音、Andrew Wang 工程/主机、Kasavin 叙事；创始人承担玩法与制作。
- 成员带走 production skill、relationship 与 taste；**没有把 EA 的团队规模和流程原样搬走**。

### Action / Maneuver
| 阶段 | 现实约束 | 创作者动作 | 外部/结果 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2009 | 离开稳定雇佣，现金 runway 有限 | 两位 founder 先起步，低工资、自筹，在家中办公 | 降低不可逆 fixed burn |
| 2009–2010 | 2 人无法包办全部作者能力 | 约九个月迭代 prototype，逐步形成七人跨专业核心 | Capability composition 与项目范围共同变化 |
| 2010 | 早期 GDC 展示市场反应不强 | 继续打磨，先向 PAX 10 玩家公开展示，再与 publisher 谈 | 先找玩家再扩大 distributor commitment |
| 2011 | 首次 XBLA/PC 商业发行与 marketing/performance义务 | 与 Warner Bros. 合作营销/认证，同时保留 IP 和自筹开发结构 | 成功发售；2011 年 11 月报道时销量 >350k（当时口径，不是终身销量） |
| 2011→2019 | 作品成功后面临扩编与工业化诱惑 | 继续维持独立、小团队和一作接一作的现金流 | 团队在 2019 回顾时七位原成员仍合作；不是“永远七人” |

### Decision Rights / Cost
- **作者项目 thesis 与 IP**：早期工作室保留。WB 合同具体 milestone / approval / recoup 未公开。
- **资金**：储蓄、自我低薪、家庭提供场地；WB 提供市场/认证等外围服务而非开发预付款。
- **能力**：项目并非 solo；七人多工种贡献不能被“两个 EA 员工”掩盖。
- **退出风险**：失败意味着储蓄消耗、友人/家人的机会成本；各成员家庭现金账单与真正退出能力 UNKNOWN。

## 3. Core Mechanism

```text
EA salary + AAA production knowledge + pre-existing creative network
      ↓
self-funded low fixed burn + borrowed household space
      ↓
AAA capability retained / AAA staffing grammar discarded
      ↓
prototypes + specialized seven-person composition
      ↓
player/press signal → distribution partner
      ↓
retained IP + next-project autonomy
```

**作者性不是通过否定专业能力得到的。它是通过把公司经验与公司目标函数拆开、把团队规格重新置于玩家体验之下得到的。**

## 4. Strongest Evidence / Boundary
- P0 Supergiant self-description: founders left EA, used household living room, remained independent.
- P1 Rao GDC China 2011: no outside development funding, severe low-salary runway, scope limited to team capabilities; PAX/IGF/distributor path.
- P1 Kasavin 2011: ~nine-month iterative prototype, no comprehensive upfront design doc.
- P1 Rao interview: WB distribution vs studio IP and development funds.
- P1 2021 Rao: father lent home and explicitly framed entrepreneurial failure as recoverable through employment.

Do not infer universal EA effect, US workplace freedoms, actual family wealth, or exact payroll/cashflow from these statements.

## 5. Creator Life / Decision Audit
- **Audit status:** SUBSTANTIAL / TEAM-LEVEL; household facts PARTIAL.
- **Life stage:** established EA professional team, leaving full-time employment in 2009.
- **Household:** founder's father offered workspace; founders' spouses/children/individual housing costs UNKNOWN.
- **Runway:** personal savings + near-zero founder wages + family-provided space; **not** outside VC/publisher advance.
- **Household burn:** UNKNOWN; explicit noncash housing/office subsidy.
- **Exit / recovery:** father expected ability to return to jobs; actual labor-market reentry cost UNKNOWN.
- **Capability vector:** design, gameplay, AAA production, art, narrative, audio, voice, console engineering.
- **Problem ownership:** HIGH; Warner Bros. control boundary PARTIAL.
- **Validation architecture:** prototype → failed/weak early public presentation → PAX 10 → publisher market/console surface → launch.
- **Reality adjudication:** STRONG iterative/product-level, but exact playtest changes partial.
- **Capability capture risk:** LOW in early Bastion; specialized abilities translated into core experience, not costly AAA pipeline.
- **Market sufficiency/legibility:** early uncertainty; strong once public interest and commercial sales established.
- **Capability scaling:** two founders → seven-person specialized core; not proof every seven-person team can replicate.
- **Major unknowns:** personal burn, exact savings, salary, WB distribution share/rights, audience funnel, outside contractors.

## 6. Cross-Case Counterfactual

- Against CASE-059 Slay the Spire: prior corporate experience is different — EA gameplay production vs Amazon QA; both retained independent tastes, neither reproduced employer’s product grammar.
- Against CASE-048 The Magic Circle: AAA exit + strong authorial fit can still lack enough market; Bastion success not proof of method's sufficiency.
- Against Chinese 王妙一: both selectively retained mature tech/production standards, chose capability-matching form.
- Against Red Hook: self-funding and authorial autonomy do not eliminate household downside.

### Anachronism Check

- 2010 PAX10, Xbox Live Arcade, IGF, Warner Bros. marketing access and XBLA certification belong to the 2009–2011 regime, not a contemporary Steam discovery formula.
- Father-provided housing/office space and low founder wages were part of this specific team’s material feasibility; cannot be assumed for later entrepreneurs.
- EA-era employment, ownership and side-project obligations must not be inferred to equal present-day Amazon/Tencent/Ubisoft or 2026 policies.
- Transferable mechanism: preserve professional competence but rebuild player thesis, team design, cash obligations and decision rights for each new context.

## 7. 2026 Transfer Status
- DURABLE: selective capability transfer, bounded fixed costs, capability-matched team, retain IP/control, demonstrate player value before expensive outside obligations.
- HISTORICAL/CONDITIONAL: 2010 PAX10, XBLA, Warner marketing channel and 2011 discovery environment.
