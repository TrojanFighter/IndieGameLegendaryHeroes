## 1. Myth / false inverse claims

- **"Game jams are only practice; no real Steam product comes out of them"** — false as an absolute: creator testimony directly traces 2024 GGJ prototype to an actually shipped March 2025 commercial title.
- **"2024 GGJ was this studio's first attempt to make games"** — false: Ivories Studios had already shipped The Handbook in June 2021; the creator says additional projects were abandoned or backlogged between titles.
- **"A Jam prototype was praised by peers, then appeared on Steam, so the team made money"** — not evidenced; local meetups are a positive product signal, not paid demand, and Steam page has no verified income or P&L.
- **"The three credited 2022 collaborators necessarily all worked for the 2025 commercial studio"** — unknown. Named credits for 2022/2023, 2024 three itch Authors, and 2025 studio developer label are different kinds of identity evidence.

## 2. Context–Situation–Action Snapshot

### 2.1 Era / Production Regime

2021–2025 small PC indie studio ecosystem: Unity-enabled jam prototypes and HTML5/Windows distribution via itch.io; multinational game-jam collaboration, Global Game Jam, local developer meetups, public Steam developer/publisher accounts. Premium boomer-shooter / retro-FPS language recognized on Steam. These tools and outlets existed at the time of action; their costs, platforms' traffic and community conversion cannot be assumed identical in 2026.

**Environment / known constraints:** founder/member job incomes, household obligations, country distribution of members, cost-of-living, Steam fee/revenue split, marketing budget and financial safety net all UNKNOWN. The 2025 self-description states members are spread across countries; exact countries and arrangement not fully verified.

### 2.2 Actor Situation

### 2.4 Anachronism Check / causal separation

Production lineage **is now creator confirmed** by contemporaneous 2024 Dec Steam announcement (E002) and 2025 launch first-person description (E003). Technical and thematic overlap alone was the earlier weak hypothesis; the addition of testimony changes the evidence tier.

Do not retrospectively assume developers knew the 2025 sale or its result in Jan 2024. **Peer feedback at a local indie meetup is not the same as preorder, crowdfunding, revenue or independent market test.** No return-on-investment claim is supportable.

## 6. Scope / innovation

Original 2024 Jam allowed pickup/merge/charge/shake/throw loops. Expansion added fuller FPS production: varied arenas vs linear/traversal stages, different combinations of baby-like weapons and effects, deliberately speedrun-friendly routes and leaderboard. Unity and Blender are present on 2024 itch metadata; exact change in technical stack across the commercial year has not been reconstructed.

The modular system is directly attributed by a developer to a teammate's contribution, but the person's public name is not specified in that source. Do not guess which 2022 named programmer wrote it.

## 7. Failure / changed plans

Creator-authored Dec 2024 Steam announcement describes other projects that did not pan out and one backlog concept that might later return, as well as The Handbook sequel idea. This is first-person evidence that the 2024 prototype was one selected direction among alternatives; not evidence of catastrophic studio failure, insolvency or a numeric 'failure rate'.

## 8. Market-access audit

**Known:** 2024 GGJ public playable page; local indie-developer meetups and reported positive feedback; Steam developer account and announcement 2024-12; product trailer/video and release-week self-promotion on Reddit; Steam launch 2025-03-27. Steam game page contains a limited number of reviews by Oct 2026.

**Unknown:** traffic, wishlist base, visibility surfaces, media reach, paid ads, creator pipeline, sale units, completion rates, revenue, profit, development budget. The local meetup feedback is **PROTOTYPE / PEER REACTION**, not verified purchasing intent.

**Do not confuse:** finished release vs market sufficiency. Different causal claims require separate evidence.

## 9. Environment / luck / counterfactual

Global Game Jam, itch and Steam supported staged public production in 2024–25. Cross-country collaborators could coordinate for a jam; the actual collaboration method and costs not disclosed. Favorable reactions at local peer gatherings may have affected continue/stop choice, according to the developer, but we lack controlled evidence isolating that reaction from the team's own conviction and work feasibility.

Counterfactual “without jam no game,” “without meetup no launch,” and “today anybody can replicate this for a year” are all UNVERIFIED.

## 10. Verdict and transfer horizon

**VERIFIED:** 2024 GGJ title is an antecedent prototype of Ivories Studios 2025 published title. This was upgraded from H after E002/E003 were retrieved.

**SUPPORTED:** team perceived modular weapon system and local peer feedback as reasons to continue. These are **participant explanations**, not causal experiments.

**NOT VERIFIED:** earnings, recoup, first-time professional career transition, precise contributors across versions, reuse of source code, individual financial upside.

**2026 Transfer status:** CONDITIONAL. The durable mechanism is that a cheap bounded experiment and immediate playable feedback can inform selection before committing substantially more production time; not that any random jam submission will become a viable full-time business.

## 12. Case sampling / why this addition is exceptional

This is **not** chosen because a news outlet featured a notable indie hit. The investigator selected *all* 18 public Week Sauce Apr 2022 submissions in 031 **before** tracking their accounts in 033, and subsequently found this team network's confirmed GGJ→Steam path via credited collaborators in 034.

The 18 project denominator belongs to **Week Sauce Apr 2022**, not to **GGJ2024**. Hence this individual follow-up path is one verified mechanism story and **cannot estimate** the conversion ratio of either jam or of independent developers overall.

This Case adds an unusually valuable low-media-visibility, genuine delivered-product mechanism with both production success and bounded financial ignorance; it is not one more famous postmortem.
